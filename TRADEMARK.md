**The code is free. The name and the logo are not.**

The licence does **not** cover these names and pictures, which belong to Steve
Gregerson / WizLabs:

- the names **"CADWiz"**, **"WizLabs"** and **"Wiz"** (as used for this product);
- the **CADWiz logo** (the blue wizard hat and gear) and the **Wiz** character
  (the same hat and gear with eyes), in every colour, size and animation.

What that means in practice:

- **Forks must use a different name and must not use the logo or the Wiz.** Call
  your version something else, and say plainly that it is based on CADWiz.
- **Don't suggest endorsement.** Don't present your project, service or product as
  official, approved or made by CADWiz or WizLabs unless it is.
- **Saying what it is is fine.** "Works with CADWiz", "a fork of CADWiz" or "based
  on the CADWiz source" are fine, as long as they are true.
- **The official CADWiz** is the one published by its maintainer: the repository
  named on [cadwiz.app/download](https://cadwiz.app/download) and the FreeCAD Addon
  Manager listing under the name CADWiz.

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, ask through
[cadwiz.app/feedback](https://cadwiz.app/feedback) before you publish.
