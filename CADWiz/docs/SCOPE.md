# What CADWiz is for (and what it isn't, yet)

## In scope

- Seeing what is open in FreeCAD: the parts, how they were built, their sizes.
- Measuring: overall size, wall thickness, openings, holes, distances, volume.
- Building and editing parts for 3D printing, as editable FreeCAD models
  (PartDesign bodies, constrained sketches, named sizes).
- Pictures first: real designs found on the web, or rough sketches, to choose from
  before anything is built in 3D.
- Marks on the model, shared between you and Claude on the CADWiz panel.
- Versions and backups: every change is a complete saved copy you can go back to.
- Print checks against your printer (bed size, overhangs, thin walls, closed
  solid) and export to STL, 3MF, STEP or FreeCAD files.
- Technical drawing sheets (front, top, side, a 3D view, sizes, a title block).
- Meshes: bringing in STL/3MF/OBJ files, repairing them, swapping a piece.

## Out of scope for now ("CADWiz 2.0")

- FEM (stress analysis), CAM (tool paths), architecture/BIM, surface modelling,
  full assembly tools.

## Never

- Deleting your files, sending your models anywhere without your say-so, or
  loosening the sandbox around the code Claude writes.
